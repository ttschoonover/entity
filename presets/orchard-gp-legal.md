*What the law asks of the Partnership, and how each obligation is met: by the software (enforced on the Record) or by an officer (a duty under the agreement). Kept by the Secretary; checked against the sources when it was written (November 2026). Not legal advice: requirements change, and an attorney or accountant should confirm each one.*

## Federal

| Law | What it requires | How the Orchard meets it |
|---|---|---|
| Securities Act of 1933; Investment Company Act of 1940 | A club whose members all actively decide its investments generally issues no securities; with passive members it may. A club whose interests are securities can still avoid registering as an investment company if it makes no public offering and has at most 100 members (section 3(c)(1)). | **Software**: every trade is a joint resolution of all partners (fund.trade_rule), never delegated; at most 99 partners (fund.max_members); participation tracked and enforced (participation module, agreement section 7); applicants are admitted only by vote, never by public offer. **Agreement**: sections 2.3, 6.2, 7. |
| Investment Advisers Act of 1940 | Paid investment advice can require registration. | **Agreement** 2.2: nobody is paid for advice or management. |
| Internal Revenue Code: Forms 1065 and K-1 | A partnership files Form 1065 by the 15th day of the third month after its tax year ends (March 15, or the next business day), and gives each partner a Schedule K-1. A six-month extension is available with Form 7004. | **Treasurer** (agreement 8.2). **Software**: the books, contributions, trades, income and expenses are on the Record; the ledger is exported for the return. |
| Centralized partnership audit regime (IRC 6221–6241) | Applies unless an eligible partnership (100 or fewer partners, all eligible, such as individuals) elects out on Schedule B-2. A partnership representative is designated. | **Treasurer**: elects out on each return while eligible; serves as partnership representative. |
| EIN | Accounts and returns in the Partnership's name use its EIN. | **Recorded** in the setting partnership.ein (sealed: partners only). Partners' own numbers stay off the Record (agreement 9.1). |
| Corporate Transparency Act (FinCEN beneficial ownership reports) | Since FinCEN's March 2025 rule, made final in 2026, entities created in the United States are exempt from beneficial ownership reporting. A general partnership is not created by a filing in any case. | Nothing to file. The Secretary re-checks if the rule changes. |
| Broker and bank customer due diligence | Accounts for a legal entity identify its beneficial owners and a controlling person. | **Treasurer** opens accounts in the Partnership's name and EIN only, with the agreement and the officers' election on the Record as evidence. |

## California

| Law | What it requires | How the Orchard meets it |
|---|---|---|
| Uniform Partnership Act of 1994 (Corporations Code 16100 and following) | A partnership exists when two or more persons carry on a business for profit as co-owners; no filing is needed. The agreement may vary most default rules, but not the duties in 16404 or the right to information in 16403. | **Agreement** (the whole of it). **Software**: admission, removal and amendment follow the agreement's rules. |
| Statement of Partnership Authority, Form GP-1 (Corporations Code 16303) | Optional. Filed with the Secretary of State ($70), it states who may act for the Partnership. | **Optional**: the partners may decide to file it if a broker or bank asks for it. |
| Fictitious business name (Business and Professions Code 17900–17930) | A partnership whose name does not include every general partner's surname files a statement with the county clerk where its principal place of business is, within 40 days of first transacting business; publishes it once a week for four weeks; files proof of publication; and refiles every five years or within 40 days after any change in the facts. Alameda County charges $40 for one name and one owner, plus $7 for each additional owner, and asks for a business mailing address. | **Secretary** (agreement 1.2, 8.3). Every partner is a registrant: new partners mean a new statement (a change in the facts). |
| Franchise Tax Board: Form 565 | A partnership doing business in California, or with California-source income, files Form 565 and gives each partner a Schedule K-1 (565). General partnerships do not pay the $800 annual tax; limited partnerships do. | **Treasurer** (agreement 8.2). Nothing to pay at the entity level. |
| Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (Civil Code 1633.7) | An electronic signature has the effect of a handwritten one when the parties agree to transact electronically. | **Agreement** 3.2; every partner signs the agreement on the Record. |
| Data breach notification (Civil Code 1798.82) | Any person or business conducting business in California that owns computerized personal information must notify affected residents of a breach of unencrypted personal information. | **Software**: the Record is sealed (encrypted); two-factor sign-in; notices sealed to each partner. **Agreement** 9: identification numbers never on the Record; officers notify after a breach. |
| California Consumer Privacy Act | Applies to businesses above its thresholds, which the Partnership is not. | Not applicable; the agreement's privacy section applies anyway. |
| Corporate Securities Law of 1968 | California's own securities law, alongside the federal one. | Same design as the federal row: active participation, no public offering. Ask a securities attorney. |
| Capacity to contract | Minors may disaffirm contracts. | **Agreement** 4.2: partners are 18 or older. |

## City of Berkeley and Alameda County

| Law | What it requires | How the Orchard meets it |
|---|---|---|
| Berkeley business license tax (Berkeley Municipal Code chapter 9.04) | The City says every person or business operating, directly or indirectly, in Berkeley needs a business license and pays an annual business license tax, applying within 30 days of starting. New licenses cost a $25 registration fee plus a minimum tax ($51 for most businesses); licenses expire December 31 and renew before March 1. A business with a Berkeley address also needs a zoning certificate. | **Secretary**: ask the Finance Department (510-981-7200, finance@berkeleyca.gov) whether a members-only investment club with no customers is "doing business" in Berkeley, record the answer here, and license if they say so. Use a mailing address rather than a co-op house as the principal office if the City requires a zoning certificate for it. |
| Alameda County fictitious business name | See the California row. | **Secretary**. |
