*What the software does not do for you. Check each item with an attorney or accountant; requirements change, and these notes are not legal or tax advice.*

## Before the first contribution
- **Partnership agreement**: have the agreement reviewed by a California attorney, fill in the bracketed blanks, enact it, and have every partner sign it on the Record.
- **Securities**: the SEC's view is that interests in a club where *every* member actively takes part in investment decisions are probably not securities; one passive member may change that. Ask a securities attorney to review section 9 and how the club actually runs.
- **EIN**: apply to the IRS for an Employer Identification Number in the Partnership's name.
- **Fictitious business name**: if the name does not include every partner's surname, file a fictitious business name statement with the county clerk.
- **Statement of Partnership Authority** (optional): filed with the Secretary of State (Form GP-1), it states who may act for the Partnership.
- **Brokerage and bank accounts**: in the Partnership's name and EIN, never in one partner's name.

## Every year
- **Federal**: Form 1065, with a Schedule K-1 for each partner.
- **California**: Form 565, with Schedule K-1 (565) for each partner. General partnerships do not pay California's $800 annual tax (limited partnerships do).
- **Partners**: each partner reports their share on their own return.
- **Elections**: hold the elections for Treasurer, Secretary and the Audit Committee before terms end.
- **Audit**: the Audit Committee reviews the books quarterly and signs checkpoints of the Record.

## Ongoing
- **Liability**: every partner is personally liable for the Partnership's debts. The agreement forbids borrowing, margin and short selling; keep it that way, and consider insurance or converting to an LLC before the club takes on any obligation.
- **Privacy**: keep tax ID numbers off the Record. California's Consumer Privacy Act applies only to businesses above its thresholds, which a club of friends will not reach, but the agreement's privacy section applies anyway.
- **Accessibility**: the website is built to WCAG 2.1 AA; offer documents in other formats on request.
