*A draft for the founding partners. It is not legal advice. Before anyone signs it, have it reviewed by a California attorney, and by a securities attorney for section 9.*

## 1. Formation and name
1. The partners form a general partnership under the California Uniform Partnership Act of 1994 (Corporations Code section 16100 and following), named **$NAME** (the "Partnership").
2. Its principal office is [ADDRESS, COUNTY], California. If its name does not include the surnames of all partners, the Treasurer files a fictitious business name statement in that county (Business and Professions Code section 17900 and following).
3. The Partnership begins on the date of its founding record and continues until dissolved under section 13.

## 2. Purpose
1. The Partnership's purpose is for its partners to learn about investing by investing together: in publicly traded securities and similar assets, decided by the partners themselves.
2. The Partnership does not borrow money, buy on margin, sell short, or write uncovered options, and takes no other position that could lose more than it has invested. (Every partner is personally liable for the Partnership's debts: see section 12.)

## 3. The record and electronic signatures
1. The Partnership keeps its decisions, memberships, offices, units and agreements on a signed, public-to-its-partners record, kept by the Athènes software (the "Record"). An act on the Record is signed with the partner's own key.
2. Each partner agrees that signing on the Record is their electronic signature, with the same effect as a handwritten one (Civil Code section 1633.7), and that notices may be given through the Record and the Partnership's website.
3. Where this agreement and the Record differ about what was decided, the Record prevails. This agreement prevails over any setting of the software.

## 4. Partners, units and certificates
1. Each partner's interest is measured in units. A partner's percentage interest is their units divided by all units outstanding.
2. A partner contributes capital in money only. Units are issued for a contribution at the unit value on the valuation date at or after the contribution is received, by resolution of the partners.
3. The unit value is the Partnership's net assets divided by the units outstanding, determined by the Treasurer at the end of each month and reviewed by the Audit Committee.
4. A partnership certificate is issued automatically from the Record to every partner when admitted and whenever their units change; the previous certificate is cancelled. A certificate is evidence of a partner's units only. It is not a security, not negotiable, and transfers nothing.

## 5. Voting
1. Each partner has one vote. Matters are decided by resolution under the rules on the Record:
   - **ordinary**: a majority of votes cast, one fifth of partners voting;
   - **joint**: a majority of the partners voting *and* a majority of the units voting, with at least half of each voting;
   - **associate**: 60% of the partners voting *and* 60% of the units voting, with at least half of each voting;
   - **organic**: two thirds of votes cast, one third of partners voting;
   - **joint-super**: two thirds of the partners voting *and* two thirds of the units voting, with at least half of each voting;
   - **constitutional**: two thirds of votes cast, half of the partners voting.

   Each resolution is decided in a single vote; no rule requires a second reading.
2. A partner votes their units with their own vote; units cannot be voted separately from the person.
3. Investment decisions (buying or selling an asset) are joint resolutions, unless the partners delegate a standing class of decisions by joint resolution.
4. **Amendment.** Any provision of this agreement may be amended, added or repealed by joint-super resolution: two thirds of the partners voting *and* two thirds of the units voting, with at least half of each voting. The text of the amendment is set out in the resolution, and the amended agreement takes effect when the resolution carries.
5. No amendment may, without the written consent of each partner affected: require a partner to contribute more capital; increase a partner's liability; take away a partner's right to withdraw under section 11 or to inspect the books under section 12; or reduce any duty below what Corporations Code section 16103 allows.

## 6. Officers and the Audit Committee
1. The officers are the **Treasurer** and the **Secretary** (membership coordinator). The **Audit Committee** has three members. Each office is filled by its own election among the partners, by approval voting, for a term of one year.
2. No partner may hold two of these offices at once, or stand for one while holding or standing for another.
3. The Treasurer keeps the books and the brokerage account in the Partnership's name, determines the unit value, prepares the Partnership's tax returns (federal Form 1065 and California Form 565, and each partner's Schedule K-1), and serves as the partnership representative under Internal Revenue Code section 6223 unless the partners designate another.
4. The Secretary receives applications, keeps the roll and minutes, sees that every new partner signs this agreement before admission, and gives notice of meetings and votes.
5. The Audit Committee reviews the Treasurer's books and the unit value each quarter and reports to the partners. It alone validates checkpoints of the Record: a checkpoint is final when a majority of its members has signed it. Its members may not be officers.
6. No officer is paid. No officer gives investment advice for compensation.

## 7. Admission
1. A person becomes a partner by applying on the Record, signing this agreement there, making their first contribution, and being admitted by joint resolution. This replaces the consent of all partners that section 16401(i) would otherwise require.
2. Every partner must be at least 18 years old.

## 8. Associations
1. The Partnership may join an association of clubs or other organizations by associate resolution (60% of the partners voting and 60% of the units voting), on written terms recorded with the resolution.
2. An association may be granted a claim on the Partnership's **profits** (a share of each distribution of profits) by joint resolution, and a claim on the Partnership's **assets** (a share of net assets on a distribution of assets or on dissolution, with any cap) by joint-super resolution. A claim ends under the rule that granted it, as the association's terms allow.
3. Claims on profits together never exceed 100% of a distribution. An association is not a partner, has no vote, and has no authority to bind the Partnership. The Partnership leaves an association by associate resolution, once its claims have ended.

## 9. Active participation
1. Every partner takes an active part in the Partnership's investment decisions: they are expected to attend meetings, study proposed investments and vote. No partner is a passive investor.
2. The partners intend that, because every partner actively manages the Partnership, their interests are not securities. If a partner cannot participate actively, they withdraw under section 11.

## 10. Allocations, distributions and accounts
1. Profits and losses are allocated among the partners in proportion to their units, after any claims of associations on profits.
2. Distributions are made only by joint resolution, in proportion to units, after the claims of associations on profits and before any claims on assets.
3. Each partner has a capital account, kept by the Treasurer.

## 11. Withdrawal
1. A partner may withdraw at any time by notice in writing on the Record. The Partnership buys their units at the unit value at the end of the month of withdrawal, less a fair share of the costs of selling assets, and pays within the time Corporations Code section 16701 requires.
2. Withdrawal does not dissolve the Partnership. A partner may be expelled only as section 16601 permits, after being heard.
3. A partner may not transfer units or any part of their interest without a joint resolution.

## 12. Duties and liability
1. Every partner owes the Partnership and the other partners the duties of loyalty and care, and acts in good faith and with fair dealing (section 16404). Nothing in this agreement reduces them below what section 16103 allows.
2. Every partner may inspect the Partnership's books and records, and the whole Record, at any time (section 16403).
3. Each partner is personally liable, jointly and severally, for the Partnership's obligations (section 16306). No partner or officer may incur any debt or obligation for the Partnership except as the partners resolve.

## 13. Dissolution
1. The Partnership is dissolved by joint-super resolution, or as section 16801 requires.
2. On winding up, assets are applied first to creditors, then to the claims of associations on assets, then to the partners in proportion to their capital accounts.

## 14. Privacy and accessibility
1. The Record and the website are open only to partners and applicants. The Partnership keeps only what it needs: names, contact details given by the partner, contributions, units and votes. Tax identification numbers are kept only by the Treasurer, off the Record, for tax filings.
2. A partner may obtain a copy of everything the Partnership holds about them, and correct it.
3. The Partnership's website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1, level AA. Any partner may ask for this agreement or any record in another format.

## 15. Disputes and general terms
1. Partners first try to settle disputes among themselves; then by mediation in [COUNTY] County; then in the courts of California.
2. This agreement is governed by the law of California. If a provision is unenforceable, the rest stands.
