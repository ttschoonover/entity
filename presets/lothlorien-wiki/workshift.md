Workshift is the labor every member contributes to running the house: cooking, cleaning, maintenance and the other jobs that keep rent low. This page summarizes the Lothlórien Workshift Policy for fall 2026 and the parts of the constitution and community agreements that touch on it [1][2][3]. The policy itself is the rule, and the Workshift Manager answers questions about it.

## Terms used on this page
- **Shift**: a weekly job on the permanent schedule, with a set number of hours.
- **Temp weeks**: weeks 1 and 2 of the semester, before the permanent schedule is posted [1].
- **Verification**: confirming that a shift was done, either by another member signing off or by photos [1].
- **Workshift Market**: the place where a member lists a shift they cannot do, so someone else can pick it up [1].
- **Blown shift**: a shift missed without being listed on the market in time [1].
- **Down hours**: the number of hours a member is behind [1].
- **Humor shift**: a set of hours each member owes once per semester, done on weekends. These are separate from the weekly shift [1].
- **Hab hours**: habitability hours, spent keeping the shared spaces clean and fit to live in [2].
- **Boarder**: a member who eats at the house and does workshift there without living there.

## What each member owes
Under the constitution each member owes 5 workshift hours a week, 3 humor hours and 3 hab hours, along with other duties, unless they have an accommodation [2]. The workshift policy sets the details [1]:
- Members with room and board owe 5 hours a week during the academic terms.
- Members who stay over a break owe half an hour for each day they are in the house, and must tell their unit manager when they will be there.
- Boarders owe between 2 and 5 hours a week, depending on their contract.
- A member away for three or more days owes no regular shifts for those days if they tell the Workshift Manager before leaving.
- The 3 humor hours are due by the end of the semester. Members verify them by sending the Workshift Manager before and after photos. Summer members who stay for only half the summer owe 1.5.

Members can ask the Workshift Manager for accommodations [1]. The Workshift Manager posts the permanent assignments by Monday of week 3 [2].

## Verifying shifts
In the two temp weeks, members sign off on a paper temp sheet with a verifier. From week 3, members use the online verification form to verify one another's shifts, and use photos when nobody can verify in person [1]. A sign-off is due within a week of the shift, and a shift left unverified after that counts as not done. A forgotten sign-off can be fixed by telling the Workshift Manager within the same week. Verifying a shift that did not happen blows the shift and adds hours owed [1].

## Missing a shift
A member who cannot do a shift lists it on the Workshift Market at least 24 hours ahead. A shift listed in time counts only as missed, and the member is docked only the hours nobody made up [1]. A shift listed late, or not at all, can be blown, and the manager may dock up to twice its length, so a blown 2-hour shift can cost 4 [1].

## Falling behind
| Hours down | What happens |
|---|---|
| 10 | a courtesy email and a meeting with the managers to plan how to catch up |
| 15 | the BSC's central office is told and the member is put on a conditional contract; the term's seniority point is lost |
| 15, already on a conditional contract | the housing contract ends automatically |
| 10, for a boarder | the boarding contract is cancelled |

Breaking a conditional contract ends the member's housing contract [1].

The fall 2026 policy has one fining date, December 21, 2026. Anyone still below zero hours that day is fined $22 for each hour, and the money goes to the house's habitability account. A member can appeal a fine to council, where it needs an absolute majority, and after that to the BSC's AdHoc committee with a preponderance of evidence [1].

## The 222 Rule
A member more than 2 hours down cannot attend Room to Room or a Special Dinner, and a Special Dinner also needs at least 2 humor hours and 2 hab hours done by the date of the event [1]. The community agreements state the same 222 Rule and apply it to one more event, which they call FO, with the humor and hab requirement applying to FO as well as Special Dinners [3].

## Social hours
Each social a member attends costs one hour of setup or cleanup. A member who skips it owes a social hour, to be made up by the next social [3].

## When you are sick
A member who is sick tells the house straight away and is excused from workshift for five days. The policy also asks them to take a COVID test as soon as they can. After five days the Workshift Manager looks at each case on its own [1].

## Paid shifts
Paid workshift is paid at $22 an hour and needs an I-9 form, the federal employment eligibility form, on file and training. Separately, during the temp weeks each house has to fill the central workshifts it is assigned for the BSC as a whole, or the house is fined [1].
