The house's own wiki. Anyone on the house Wi-Fi can read it; members can edit any page, and every version is kept.

## Writing here
Pages are Markdown: `## Heading`, `- list item`, `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `[link](https://…)`. A footnote is `[^1]` where you cite it and `[^1]: the source` on its own line at the end.

## Alerts
Members get the house's alerts on their phone or laptop and by email. Turn them on under **Alerts**.
