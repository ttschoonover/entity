Lothlórien sits on the Hayward Fault, which runs past its back yard.[^1] Between 2009 and 2013 the BSC rebuilt the structure of both houses to survive the next big earthquake, and in between, in 2010, the elves put the first solar panels in the BSC on the South House roof.[^1][^2][^3]

## The fault in the back yard
![Fault creep in California Memorial Stadium](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Hayward_fault_separation_Memorial_Stadium.jpg "Memorial Stadium, a few blocks from the house, pulled apart by slow creep on the Hayward Fault.")

The Hayward Fault is a branch of the San Andreas system. It runs through the east side of the UC Berkeley campus, straight through the field at Memorial Stadium, and the Berkeley Seismological Laboratory calls it the most dangerous fault in the Bay Area.[^4] No other major university has a fault this dangerous running through its campus.[^5]

It moves two ways. Most of the time it creeps a few millimeters a year, and over the decades that has offset the two halves of the stadium by more than a foot.[^6][^7] Every so often it breaks. The last time was at 7:53 in the morning on October 21, 1868, a magnitude 6.8 quake that was called "the great San Francisco earthquake" until 1906.[^8][^9] Trenches dug across the fault show big quakes every 95 to 183 years, about 150 on average. More than 150 years have passed since 1868, and the chance of another like it within thirty years is about 30 percent.[^8][^10]

## The North House, 2009
The BSC rated the North House high-risk and retrofitted it in the summer of 2009, paid for with the BSC's retrofit bonds.[^1] It cost $537,000, about $19,000 per resident, on a 40-year bond.[^11]

## The solar panels, 2010
In September 2010 the house put a 7.92-kilowatt solar array on the South House roof, and it began producing on December 1, 2010.[^2] It cost $39,611:[^2]

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| Five-year loan from the BSC central office, at 5.25% | $24,000 |
| House Approved Projects (the house's own budget) | $13,266 |
| Raised by members | $2,345 |
| **Total** | **$39,611** |

Lothlórien was the first BSC house with solar panels.[^3] Alfred Twu, a resident then, worked out in 2011 whether the array paid for itself.[^2]

## The South House, 2013
The South House was retrofitted in the summer of 2013. The work closed its kitchen, so residents got a 33 percent rent rebate, to about $350 a month.[^1] A third of the house ate at Wolf, Oscar Wilde, Davis and Castro, and the African-American Theme House shared its kitchen and laundry.[^1] Council met every other week, and with no dishes to wash, workshift was assigned by area of the house instead of by hours.[^1]

## Living with it
- The walls, foundations and connections holding both houses together date from 2009 and 2013, even where the rooms look a century old.
- The house paid for its own power, mostly with a loan and its own budget. It can do it again.[^2]
- In an earthquake: drop, cover and hold on. Know where the gas shutoff is, and keep water and a flashlight in your room.
