# Charter

Founding texts are addressable to the paragraph. Changing this file requires a
constitutional decision; `entity gate` checks it.

## § 1  The entity

¹ This entity is the record held in `record/events.jsonl` and everything that
record determines.

² Its name is the value of `entity.name` in the parameter file.

## § 2  Membership

¹ Anyone may apply. Whether an application takes effect on recording or waits
for admission is the value of `membership.admission`.

² Membership cannot be transferred, sold or inherited.

³ Anyone may leave at any time. Nothing in this charter, or in any decision made
under it, can prevent that.

## § 3  Decisions

¹ Each member has one vote, whatever they hold.

² A decision carries when the quorum of its rule votes and the threshold of its
rule of the decisive votes are in favour, and more are in favour than against.

³ The figures in ² are those in the parameter file. The code reads them there,
and there only. Altering them here without altering them there changes nothing.

## § 4  The record

¹ A record, once made, is never altered.

² Any member may verify the record, and may refuse to act on a copy that does
not verify.
