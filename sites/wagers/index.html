Four ways to bet

All staked in points, held in escrow on the record until the result is in.

  • Pool

    Pick an outcome. Everything staked goes into one pot, and the winners share it in proportion to what they put in.

    …

  • Over/under

    A number to beat. Bet over or under; if it lands exactly on the line, everyone gets their stake back.

    …

  • Challenge

    Call out one member, or anyone: “my 50 says I’m right, against your 30.” The loser can simply concede.

    …

  • Market

    Buy and sell shares in outcomes. Each winning share pays out; the price at any moment is what the club thinks the odds are.

The arbiter

Elected by the members to call results. The arbiter never bets while in office, never judges a bet they hold a stake in, and every result can be appealed to a vote.
