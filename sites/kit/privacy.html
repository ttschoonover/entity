The public record

The network keeps a public record of everything its members do: joining, voting, proposing, lending, standing for office. Each entry is signed by the person who made it and linked to the one before, so no one can change or remove an entry without it showing.

This is how the network works, and it means that your id, the name you choose, your public key and everything you sign are public and permanent. Anyone can download the record, and copies may be kept by other people. You are told this, and asked to confirm it, before you join. You can use a pseudonym.

Cookies and storage

The only cookie is gr_session, which keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so it needs no consent. Everything else listed above stays in your browser and is never sent to us. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

You can clear everything this site stores in your browser with the button under “Your data”, or from your browser’s settings.

What we never do

We do not sell or share personal data, show advertising, track you across sites, or build profiles. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request not to sell or share your data, which we do not do anyway.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA (California) give you rights over your personal data. Here is how each works on this site.

  • See your data. “Download my data” gives you everything the record holds about you, as a file. For your email address, if you gave one, write to us.
  • Correct it. You can change the name others see at any time. Your id cannot change.
  • Delete it. We delete your email address when you turn two-factor off or ask us. You can leave the network at any time, and clear your browser. We cannot remove what you signed from the public record: it is linked to everything after it, and copies are held by others. If you need something hidden from this website for a serious reason, write to us and we will do what we can.
  • Take it with you. The download is plain JSON, and the whole record is open for anyone to copy.
  • Object or withdraw consent. You can stop at any time by leaving. What you already signed stays on the record.
  • Complain. You can complain to your data protection authority.

We do not treat anyone differently for using these rights.

Children

This site is not meant for children. You must be 16 or older to join, and you are asked to confirm your age when you join. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has joined, write to us and we will remove what we can and stop them signing.
