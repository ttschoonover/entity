The Solidarity Network

Neighbours who lend each other what they own. Borrow a drill, a tent or a sewing machine against a small cash deposit, returned when the thing comes back. If a deposit is out of reach, the solidarity fund stands behind it, and no one asks why.

How borrowing works

Every step is signed on the public record, so the books of every deposit keep themselves.

  1. Ask. Find something in the library and ask to borrow it. Tick the box if you want the solidarity fund to cover the deposit.
  2. Meet. The lender hands it over and signs that they received your deposit, in cash, or none if the fund covers it.
  3. Return. Bring it back on time. The lender signs that it came back and returns the deposit, or says on the record what was kept and why.
  4. Settle. If you think too much was kept, dispute it within the time allowed. The Solidarity Committee decides.
